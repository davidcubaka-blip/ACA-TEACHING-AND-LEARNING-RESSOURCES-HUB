Week 4 — Classroom Activities (Food & Market)

  • Warm-up: Flashcard drill — 5 minutes
  • Pronunciation: Practice partitives (du / de la / de l' / des)
  • Role-play: Market transaction in pairs (seller / buyer). Use phrases: "Je voudrais...", "Combien ça coûte ?", "C'est trop cher !"
  • Listening: Play the restaurant dialogue. Students answer comprehension questions.
  • Homework: Record yourself ordering at the market (30–60s) and upload.
